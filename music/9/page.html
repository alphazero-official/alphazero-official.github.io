--- layout: release title: CARAMEL id: 9 comments: true image: /music/9/cover.jpg description: 1oolm - CARAMEL is the ninth single released under alpha.zero. --- CARAMEL is the ninth single released under alpha.zero and the fifth solo project by 1oolm.

The single was released digitally on October 11th, 2025 on YouTube and SoundCloud.

This release is preceded by TIMELOOP and followed by OUROBOROS.


As a major comeback record in preparation for the OUROBOROS album, CARAMEL takes inspiration from OUROBOROS' cyclical storytelling and compresses it by a factor of ten.
This track marks an evolutionary step for alpha.zero towards a greater variety of electronic music, mellowing out from hardcore and upgrading to a grander palette of self-expression.


THOUGHTS


Finishing OUROBOROS got me in an entire mood to vent out feelings in bizarre music.
You can tell because there are swear words, seemingly the first in alpha.zero's existence.

Here, I tackle cycles of hard, carnal addiction: drugs, alcohol, sex, you name it.
My personal takeaway is the sexual deviancy I've faced growing up online.

The track is split into four loose story beats:


  1. HELL: fighting the urge to relapse
  2. HIGH: "screw it" and the haze of the early high
  3. CLOUD 9: pure, concentrated dopamine
  4. HANGOVER: the consequences

The cover art is essentially a blanket covering the topics at hand:
  • The described object is Pervitin: an early form of methamphetamine. Substance abuse, check.
  • The only visible letters in the image are "Perv". Sexual deviancy, check.
  • The trans flag is subtly overlaid in the background. Go figure.
    (During my time in denial, I've had a lot of self-doubt about my transition being genuine, thinking that it's baseless, and simply in the name of perverse fantasy.)
You can make something out of the way the image is framed, but I wasn't thinking of that.
