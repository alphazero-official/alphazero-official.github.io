--- layout: release title: OUROBOROS id: 10 comments: true image: /music/10/cover.jpg description: OUROBOROS is 1oolm's debut album, coming November 15th. --- OUROBOROS is 1oolm's debut album.

The album was released digitally on November 15th, 2025 on Youtube, SoundCloud and Bandcamp.

This release is preceded by CARAMEL.


As a story-driven take on a diverse array of hardcore genres, OUROBOROS incorporates themes of love, hate, forgiveness, abandonment, identity, self-loathing, and repetition in one looping tracklist, bringing more deliberate emotion into music mostly relegated to dancefloors.
