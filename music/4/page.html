--- layout: release title: Devastation id: 4 comments: true image: /music/4/cover.jpg description: Devastation is the fourth single released under alpha.zero and the second collaboration project between 1oolm and Stretched Across Infinity. --- Devastation is the fourth single released under alpha.zero and the second collaboration project between 1oolm and Stretched Across Infinity.

The single was released digitally on September 28th, 2023 on 6 streaming platforms.

This release is preceded by tomorrowcave and followed by Constraint (High Mass Remix).



As the second release in the duology of mobile production, Devastation provides a stark contrast to tomorrowcave, with hard frenchcore drops to blow some heads off.

Trivia


  • This song was originally meant for an EP called FACTOR Y. The remnants of that project can be seen in the cover art, with "FACTORY#4" written above the "DEVASTATION" text, and the wrench-in-hand image itself being on FACTOR Y's cover.
  • This song was made entirely on a phone.
