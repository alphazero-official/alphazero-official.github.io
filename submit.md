<header>
    <h1>MUSIC SUBMISSIONS</h1>
</header>
<div id="line-x"><hr /></div>
You can send the label some music that you're proud of and would like to get out there.  
<br>
<h2>WHAT MUSIC?</h2>
Usually, the label seeks electronic-styled talents and elaborate pieces of work. Principles lay in quality over quantity: releases are given time to breathe, a week at minimum.  

Genres/scenes I have particular interest/experience with include:
- DTM/"rhythm game music" (artcore, techcore, complextro, hardtek, j-core, etc.)
- Breaks (ambient dnb, jungle, breakcore, mashcore, etc.)
- European Harder-Styles (hardstyle, frenchcore, uptempo, etc.)
- Hyperflip/Dariacore (crazy)

Some no-no acts include:
- AI-generated media (visuals and audio)
- NSFW/NSFL and controversial content (overtly political material, graphic sexual/gore material etc.)  
<br>
<h2>REQUIREMENTS</h2>
You will need: 
- Basic metadata: artist name(s), album/track name(s), genre.
- Track(s) in lossless (.wav/.flac) format
- Cover art in 3000x3000 resolution or above. (if you have none, we can make something)
- Proof of legitimacy (music is not AI - a project screenshot is enough)  
- A preferred SNS/website to be attributed by (anywhere from 1 to 3)  
<br>
<h2>RELEASE TERMS</h2>
The baseline values are: you own it, I endorse, catalog and promote it.  
As of October 2026, alpha.zero doesn't have the resources for streaming distribution or physical media. Hence, you're basically signing up for a spotlight: the uploads are still yours, they're simply slightly branded, assisted with and sent off to the alpha.zero brand. You keep 100% of the profit and ownership, though alpha.zero may still use your work as part of the label (re-issues, compilations etc. done with prior notice)  

Everything can be discussed and worked out individually.
