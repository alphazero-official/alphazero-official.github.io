Yet another blow to the availability of our music. How preposterous. I swear this wasn't meant to be.<br>
Spotify, Apple Music and TIDAL support is gone, as I've parted ways with my distributor, hopefully temporarily.<br> 
This came to be when they introduced document verification into their website. I had my doubts about whipping out my passport, but I eventually decided to do it. Turns out, Veriff is a horrible experience. I ended up spending an *hour and a half* dealing with an extremely unreliable system, the issues consisting of 20 different ways of the site giving up and not letting me progress. My perseverance managed to get me into the meat of things, but lo and behold, my document couldn't verify me. I checked, and their site says it absolutely can. I have no explanation for this. I've tried for a few days since this incident, but to no avail.<br><br>

TL;DR: Document verification system is hot garbage, and that wall is not letting me distribute my release.<br>
In fact, I got so miffed I went to Roblox to have them verify my passport, and it worked <span id="shytext">(relatively)</span> *flawlessly*. Hope this gets sorted out eventually.<br><br>

What this means for the near future is that I'll only be uploading alpha.zero music on Soundcloud and Youtube. Business continues as usual, and when we're back I'll get all the missed tracks onto the streaming services.<br><br>
I will exercise this ability now. Catch the premiere July 20.
